How it works

  • Your cursor is you. Move the mouse (or drag a finger, like a trackpad) to walk around. Everyone sees everyone, live.
  • Click to act: toss a coin in the fountain, paint a tile, plant a flower, type on a laptop, start a game.
  • Enter opens the chat. Only people near you can read your message, and it fades in seconds.
  • 1 to 5 show emotes. F opens the album, M toggles sound, Esc opens the menu.
  • Explore. Bandstand, fountain, playground, pitch, coworking deck, arcade, promenade. Some things are never announced.

About the chat. Messages are not stored. Links, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and profanity are replaced with "•••" automatically, but no filter is perfect: do not share personal details. You can turn the chat off in the menu.

Little Plaza is an independent project. The genre — a shared place where every visitor is a cursor — became widely known through Cursor Camp by Neal Agarwal (neal.fun). The name, art, map, sounds and code here are our own.
